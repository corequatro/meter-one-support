Privacy Policy for Meter One

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Meter One is a photo light meter app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how Meter One handles information when you use the app.

Data Collection

Meter One does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require user registration, does not create user accounts, and does not ask you to provide personal information.

Camera Access

Meter One may request access to the iPhone camera in order to evaluate light in the scene and calculate exposure values. Camera data is processed locally on your device. Meter One does not upload photos, video, camera frames, or camera data to any server.

Photos and Media

Meter One does not access your photo library unless a future version of the app explicitly asks for permission and explains why. Meter One does not save or upload your photos.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Meter One does not use third-party advertising services. Meter One does not track users across apps or websites. Meter One does not use third-party analytics services.

Location Data

Meter One does not collect or use your location data.

Data Sharing

Because Meter One does not collect personal data, it does not share personal data with third parties.

Children’s Privacy

Meter One does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
